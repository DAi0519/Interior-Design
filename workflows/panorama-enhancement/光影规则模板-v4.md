# 全景光影规则模板 v4

v4在历史v3前新增一条照明重建指令，并只修改原第1、3、6、25、27条，其余22条逐字保留。保护结构、来源内容与材质身份，同时允许替换与目标冲突的时段、光色、曝光、直射阳光、日斑和投影。默认保留原光影的v4资产不变；本规则仅用于模板或自定义换光影分支。

- 第1条：保护的是物理边界，避免将照明和阴影边界冻结。
- 第3条：保留原图可辨细节的等级与简单外观，亮度服从目标；暗玻璃与模糊反射不得补清不可辨内容的v3句子逐字保留。
- 第6条：固定的是材质本色和不透明度，目标光色与明暗分区可以改变可见颜色与亮度，不要求复制原图光照或调色。
- 第25条：仅目标下仍明亮的远景需要高光滚降；窗户及外景明暗应遵照目标时段，不能锁住白天亮度。
- 第27条：用户光影补充优先于模板偏好；结构、来源内容和材质身份固定，原图照明、曝光与投影不固定。

实际组装为可选用户补充、所选卡片全文、以下公共保护规则；先给具体光影目标，再限定结构与来源内容。无卡片的自定义光影仍为用户补充、公共规则。Flux最后加一次zhiganEnhance，OneAPI不加触发词。规则正文及其SHA不因编译顺序变化而改变，最终送模词另按全文计算SHA并归档。

```text
Rebuild the illumination to match the lighting target; replace the input's time of day, light color, direct sunlight, sun patches, and cast-shadow patterns wherever they conflict with that target. Then preserve scene content as follows. Preserve the input's existing geometry, layout, camera perspective, physical boundaries, and object placement. Do not add, remove, move, or complete any structure. When an area is distant, dark, blurred, or ambiguous, preserve its original level of discernible detail and simple appearance, while allowing brightness to follow the lighting target. In dark or blurred glass and reflections, exposure changes must not reveal, reconstruct, or sharpen content that is not already clearly visible in the input. Do not reinterpret ambiguous areas. Treat every existing surface as continuous and unchanged unless its boundary is clearly visible in the input. Preserve intrinsic material color and opacity, not the input's illumination color or color grade; allow the target's light colors and light-and-shadow separation to change apparent surface color and brightness while retaining material identity. Treat indoor transparent and reflective surfaces as existing interior surfaces. Preserve their original content and opacity, while allowing brightness and the intensity of already discernible reflections to follow the lighting target. Do not create new reflected objects, luminaires, interior layouts, or spaces behind glass, including when the target is darker outside and brighter indoors. A bright, translucent, or reflective area is not evidence of additional visible content. Keep white balance and warm-and-cool variation consistent with the lighting target, while preserving original material color identity. Preserve readable shadow detail and smooth highlight roll-off. Keep the tonal range natural and photographic. More clearly increase tonal separation within existing materials while preserving smooth transitions and the lighting target's overall exposure. Let already-visible form turning, fabric relief, wood grain, stone variation, and metal response read with greater tactile depth without adding pattern or surface detail. Enrich the existing color density, tonal layering, and diffuse-to-specular response of materials with an irregular camera-captured character, avoiding clean synthetic sheen while preserving their original color identity and overall balance. Refine existing luminance layers across already visible materials, with smooth gradual transitions. Improve gentle color differentiation between existing materials while preserving their original identities. Refine the smooth transition between existing highlights and shadows, allowing their extent to follow the lighting target. Build a richer, more immersive sense of the target ambient light through gentle but more legible tonal gradation across visible material forms, with no extra visible content. Preserve existing subtle natural unevenness in material response and tonal variation. Favor restrained camera-captured irregularity over uniform polish, idealized clarity, perfectly smooth gradients, or showroom-like surfaces. Keep local contrast and edge definition restrained, retaining natural irregularity and gentle optical softness in existing fine repetitive textures and distant detail. Avoid simulated render crispness, pristine edge separation, and overly controlled tonal shaping. Keep overall color saturation consistent with the lighting target, while preserving original material color identity and balance. In distant regions that remain bright under the lighting target, retain broad exposure roll-off with slightly reduced color separation, without recovering detail. Let window and exterior brightness follow the target time of day and exposure rather than preserving their input daytime brightness. Use existing windows, openings, and luminaires; do not invent light fixtures or openings. Apply the preset lighting target and then the user's refinements; user refinements override the preset lighting preferences while geometry, scene content, and intrinsic material identity remain fixed. The input's illumination, exposure, and shadow patterns are not fixed.
```
