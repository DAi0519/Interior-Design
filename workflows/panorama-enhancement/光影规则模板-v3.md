# 全景光影规则模板 v3

v3只改v2的第3、8条数组项，其余25条逐字保持。默认v4、共享11卡、模型参数及尾触发词方式均不变。

- 暗玻璃/模糊反射：曝光变化不能作为恢复或锐化原图不可辨内容的依据。
- 反射：仅允许原图已可辨反射调强弱；禁止夜间内亮外暗时补出新的反射物体、灯具、布局或玻璃后空间。

以下为公共规则正文；实际组装仍为可选用户补充、公共规则、所选卡片全文，Flux最后加一次 zhiganEnhance。

```text
Preserve the input's existing geometry, layout, camera perspective, boundaries, and object placement. Do not add, remove, move, or complete any structure. When an area is distant, dark, blurred, or ambiguous, retain its original visibility and tonal simplicity. In dark or blurred glass and reflections, exposure changes must not reveal, reconstruct, or sharpen content that is not already clearly visible in the input. Do not reinterpret ambiguous areas. Treat every existing surface as continuous and unchanged unless its boundary is clearly visible in the input. Preserve intrinsic surface color and opacity, while allowing brightness to follow the lighting target. Treat indoor transparent and reflective surfaces as existing interior surfaces. Preserve their original content and opacity, while allowing brightness and the intensity of already discernible reflections to follow the lighting target. Do not create new reflected objects, luminaires, interior layouts, or spaces behind glass, including when the target is darker outside and brighter indoors. A bright, translucent, or reflective area is not evidence of additional visible content. Keep white balance and warm-and-cool variation consistent with the lighting target, while preserving original material color identity. Preserve readable shadow detail and smooth highlight roll-off. Keep the tonal range natural and photographic. More clearly increase tonal separation within existing materials while preserving smooth transitions and the lighting target's overall exposure. Let already-visible form turning, fabric relief, wood grain, stone variation, and metal response read with greater tactile depth without adding pattern or surface detail. Enrich the existing color density, tonal layering, and diffuse-to-specular response of materials with an irregular camera-captured character, avoiding clean synthetic sheen while preserving their original color identity and overall balance. Refine existing luminance layers across already visible materials, with smooth gradual transitions. Improve gentle color differentiation between existing materials while preserving their original identities. Refine the smooth transition between existing highlights and shadows, allowing their extent to follow the lighting target. Build a richer, more immersive sense of the target ambient light through gentle but more legible tonal gradation across visible material forms, with no extra visible content. Preserve existing subtle natural unevenness in material response and tonal variation. Favor restrained camera-captured irregularity over uniform polish, idealized clarity, perfectly smooth gradients, or showroom-like surfaces. Keep local contrast and edge definition restrained, retaining natural irregularity and gentle optical softness in existing fine repetitive textures and distant detail. Avoid simulated render crispness, pristine edge separation, and overly controlled tonal shaping. Keep overall color saturation consistent with the lighting target, while preserving original material color identity and balance. In already bright distant regions, retain broad exposure roll-off with slightly reduced color separation, without recovering detail. Use existing windows, openings, and luminaires; do not invent light fixtures or openings. Apply the preset target and then the user's refinements; user refinements override the preset lighting preferences while the geometry and material constraints above remain fixed.
```
