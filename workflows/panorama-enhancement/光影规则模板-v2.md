# 全景光影规则模板 v2

本版替换旧的六句公共规则。以默认v4的27句为底稿，17句原文及顺序保留，8句局部调整照明限制，删除固定漫射日光和抬中间调两句。默认v4本身不改写；光影卡片仍使用各自原文。

## 完整拼接模板

花括号替换为实际内容。用户未填写补充时，删除整个用户段及标题；仅有自定义要求时，省略卡片段。Flux在整个最终提示词末尾保留一次 zhiganEnhance；OneAPI不添加。

```text
[USER LIGHTING REFINEMENTS]
{用户光影要求；未填写时删除本段及标题}

Preserve the input's existing geometry, layout, camera perspective, boundaries, and object placement. Do not add, remove, move, or complete any structure. When an area is distant, dark, blurred, or ambiguous, retain its original visibility and tonal simplicity. Do not reinterpret ambiguous areas. Treat every existing surface as continuous and unchanged unless its boundary is clearly visible in the input. Preserve intrinsic surface color and opacity, while allowing brightness to follow the lighting target. Treat indoor transparent and reflective surfaces as existing interior surfaces. Preserve their original content and opacity, while allowing brightness and reflection intensity to follow the lighting target. A bright, translucent, or reflective area is not evidence of additional visible content. Keep white balance and warm-and-cool variation consistent with the lighting target, while preserving original material color identity. Preserve readable shadow detail and smooth highlight roll-off. Keep the tonal range natural and photographic. More clearly increase tonal separation within existing materials while preserving smooth transitions and the lighting target's overall exposure. Let already-visible form turning, fabric relief, wood grain, stone variation, and metal response read with greater tactile depth without adding pattern or surface detail. Enrich the existing color density, tonal layering, and diffuse-to-specular response of materials with an irregular camera-captured character, avoiding clean synthetic sheen while preserving their original color identity and overall balance. Refine existing luminance layers across already visible materials, with smooth gradual transitions. Improve gentle color differentiation between existing materials while preserving their original identities. Refine the smooth transition between existing highlights and shadows, allowing their extent to follow the lighting target. Build a richer, more immersive sense of the target ambient light through gentle but more legible tonal gradation across visible material forms, with no extra visible content. Preserve existing subtle natural unevenness in material response and tonal variation. Favor restrained camera-captured irregularity over uniform polish, idealized clarity, perfectly smooth gradients, or showroom-like surfaces. Keep local contrast and edge definition restrained, retaining natural irregularity and gentle optical softness in existing fine repetitive textures and distant detail. Avoid simulated render crispness, pristine edge separation, and overly controlled tonal shaping. Keep overall color saturation consistent with the lighting target, while preserving original material color identity and balance. In already bright distant regions, retain broad exposure roll-off with slightly reduced color separation, without recovering detail. Use existing windows, openings, and luminaires; do not invent light fixtures or openings. Apply the preset target and then the user's refinements; user refinements override the preset lighting preferences while the geometry and material constraints above remain fixed.

[LIGHTING PRESET]
{所选光影模板完整提示词} zhiganEnhance
```
